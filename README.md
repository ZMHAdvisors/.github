
# ZMH Advisors

**Powering Vantage, a shareholder intelligence and corporate governance research platform.**

## About Us

ZMH Advisors specializes in shareholder intelligence, corporate governance research, and data-driven solutions that help organizations make informed decisions.

## Vantage by ZMH Advisors

**Vantage** is our shareholder intelligence platform, bringing together ownership insights, institutional voting activity, shareholder meeting results, and governance research into a centralized experience.

Our platform helps users:

- **Explore shareholder ownership:** Understand institutional ownership and investor relationships.
- **Analyze proxy voting:** Research voting decisions, patterns, and institutional behavior.
- **Monitor shareholder meetings:** Review annual and special meeting outcomes.
- **Access governance insights:** Explore shareholder proposals, engagement activity, and corporate governance developments.
- **Make informed decisions:** Turn complex shareholder data into actionable intelligence.

## Our Mission

To make shareholder and governance intelligence more accessible, transparent, and actionable through technology and research.
