## About ZMH Advisors

ZMH Advisors focuses on shareholder intelligence, corporate governance research, and data-driven insights that support informed decision-making.

## Introducing Vantage

**Vantage by ZMH Advisors** is a shareholder intelligence platform that brings ownership information, institutional proxy voting activity, shareholder meeting results, and governance research together in one place.

Designed to make complex information easier to explore and understand, Vantage helps users evaluate shareholder behavior and governance developments with greater clarity.

## What Vantage Offers

- **Shareholder ownership insights:** Explore institutional ownership and investor relationships.
- **Proxy voting intelligence:** Research institutional voting decisions, trends, and rationales.
- **Shareholder meeting analysis:** Review annual and special meeting results.
- **Corporate governance research:** Examine shareholder proposals and governance developments.
- **Company and investor insights:** Bring together relevant information for more informed analysis and reporting.

## Our Mission

To make shareholder and corporate governance intelligence more accessible, transparent, and actionable through research and technology.
